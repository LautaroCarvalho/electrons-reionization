### Notes on two corrections to `igm_losses.py`

1. The module docstring attributes the collisional-excitation cross-sections to
   *"Stone & Kim (2002), J. Phys. B 35, 1675"*. The correct reference is
   **Stone, Kim & Desclaux (2002), J. Res. NIST 107, 327**
   (doi:10.6028/jres.107.026); the manuscript uses the corrected form.

2. `_mean_secondary_energy` originally used an ad-hoc secondary spectrum
   ∝ 1/(1+(ε/8)^2.1). The true BEB differential cross-section
   (**Kim & Rudd 1994**), rebuilt from the module's own `_DIPOLE_FIT`
   coefficients, gives ⟨ε⟩ larger by 3–29%, and therefore
   Λ_ion = σ_ion·(B+⟨ε⟩) larger by up to 20%. This is now
   switch `FIX_SECONDARY_SPECTRUM` in the module, default `True`, following the
   same pattern as `FIX_KN_KERNEL` etc.
